# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jesse Lesperance ("I," "me," "my") collects, uses, and protects information through the website lesperance.io (the "Site") and the Jesse Lesperance AI Agent Alerts SMS program (the "SMS Program").

## Information I collect

- **Website:** The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical information, such as IP addresses, in its server logs. If you contact me through the Site or by email, I receive whatever information you choose to send, such as your name and email address.
- **SMS Program:** When you opt in, I collect your mobile phone number, the keyword you texted (EVENT or DAILY), the date and time you opted in and confirmed, your message history with the program, and your opt-out requests.

## How I use information

I use SMS Program information only to:

- send the AI agent event alerts and/or daily digests you subscribed to;
- respond to HELP requests and other questions;
- process opt-out (STOP) requests and keep a record of your consent and opt-out status; and
- run, maintain, and troubleshoot the SMS Program.

## SMS and mobile information sharing

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your mobile phone number is never sold or rented.

The only exception is service providers that help deliver the messages, such as Twilio, my SMS messaging provider, and mobile carriers. They process your phone number and message content only as needed to send messages for the SMS Program.

## Other disclosures

I may disclose information if the law requires it, or if it's needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of me, my users, or others.

## Data retention

I keep SMS Program records, including consent and opt-out records, for as long as you are subscribed and for a reasonable time after that to meet legal and carrier compliance requirements.

## Your choices

- **Opt out of SMS:** Reply STOP to any message at any time.
- **Get help:** Reply HELP to any message or email {{ site.sms_contact_email }}.
- **Access or deletion:** Email {{ site.sms_contact_email }} to ask what information I hold about you or to have it deleted, subject to any legal record-keeping requirements.

## Security

I use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Children

The Site and the SMS Program are not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised.
